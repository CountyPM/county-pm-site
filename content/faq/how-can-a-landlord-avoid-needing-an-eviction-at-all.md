Screen carefully on the way in, serve precise notices, and talk to the tenant within days of a late payment. Most evictions are expensive failures of one of those three things, and the first is the cheapest to get right.

Use consistent written screening criteria and apply them to every applicant. Collect rent on a predictable schedule and follow up immediately on a miss, not after the second month. Early conversations resolve many situations through a payment plan or a referral to rental assistance before anyone files anything. If a notice is truly needed, get it right, because a defective notice hands the other side a defense and restarts your clock.

Annual inspections catch maintenance and lease problems while they are measured in weeks instead of seasons. And when a tenancy cannot be saved, a negotiated move-out can cost less than a contested case. Prevention is not softness; it is the lowest-cost way to manage a property.
