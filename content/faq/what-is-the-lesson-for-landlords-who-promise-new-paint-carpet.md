Deliver what you promise, or don't promise it. When a lease is signed on the strength of new paint, new carpet, professional cleaning, and pool service, those aren't extras — they're the deal. Hand over a unit that falls short and you can lose a high-quality, pays-on-time resident in a single weekend.

The cost of that broken move-in is rarely just the disappointment. An owner who runs out of time can face a refund claim, possible damages, and the very outcome the whole effort was meant to avoid: a vacancy and a fresh search for a tenant. The money and goodwill lost dwarf what it would have taken to finish the work.

The practical rule for Ventura County owners is to promise only what you'll actually complete before keys change hands, and to build in margin so a late vendor doesn't blow up the move-in. Under-promise and over-deliver on turn work — finishing the punch list is almost always cheaper than replacing a good resident you just lost.
