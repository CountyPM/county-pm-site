No. The familiar "income must be three times the rent" standard is an industry screening guideline, not a law — there's no California or federal statute that fixes a required income-to-rent ratio for private rentals. Owners and managers adopt it as a rule of thumb, and some local affordable-housing programs even limit how rigidly such ratios can be applied.

Treating it as an unbendable rule can also work against you. A single professional at 2.5x with a long record of on-time payments and low expenses can be a lower-risk tenant than a larger household sitting right at 3x with more obligations and more wear on the unit. The ratio is a useful starting point, not the whole picture, and the more reliable approach is to weigh income alongside payment history, references, and stability — applying the same criteria to every applicant to stay on the right side of fair-housing law.
