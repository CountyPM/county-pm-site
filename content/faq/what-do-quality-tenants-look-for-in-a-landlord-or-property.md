Mainly, they want repairs done promptly and properly by qualified professionals. A quality tenant is judging how problems will be handled long before anything breaks.

Beyond repairs, they look for a clear plan for emergencies, including who answers after hours; an easy way to pay rent online with a record of every transaction; and fair, professional handling of the security deposit and their privacy. Owners who can show these things at the showing tend to attract and keep better applicants than those who say they will figure it out when the time comes.
