In our experience, no. Zillow generally displays one version of a property at a time, so an active for-sale listing has to be removed before a rental listing will post, and if both versions exist the for-sale listing is the one that shows. Listing-site rules change, so check the current behavior before you plan around it.

That is one more reason to pick a primary objective instead of marketing a home for sale and for rent simultaneously. If you decide to switch from selling to renting, take the sale listing down first, then publish the rental listing with the lease term you actually want.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice; confirm your situation with a qualified professional.
