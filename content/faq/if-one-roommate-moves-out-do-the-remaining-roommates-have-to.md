Usually, yes. When roommates apply together, the owner approves a household, not three separate people: the combined income, credit and rental history were evaluated as a unit. When one person leaves, the reasonable question is whether the household that remains would still be approved on its own.

Three incomes may cover the rent comfortably where two do not, and one roommate's strong credit may have offset another's thin file. So expect the owner or manager to ask the remaining tenants for current income and information and check them against the same standards used at move-in. If they still qualify, the change is documented in writing and the tenancy continues.

If they don't, there are two ways forward: a qualified replacement applies and joins the agreement, or the tenancy ends and the home is re-rented. Making that call when the roommate leaves is far better than discovering three months later that the rent no longer gets paid in full. Whatever the outcome, apply the same written criteria you'd apply to any applicant.
