Yes. Many management firms offer a lease-up-only service: they market the home, screen applicants, and sign the lease, then hand the property back to you. If you want to self-manage after move-in, that is a legitimate way to get a professionally screened tenant without a long-term management contract.

The trade-off is on the applicant side. Some of the strongest applicants ask who will handle repairs and emergencies before they commit, and some will decline an otherwise good home once they learn the owner will be the one answering the phone after move-in. If you go this route, be ready to show a real plan: licensed, insured vendors, a written emergency procedure, and an easy way to pay rent online. A lease-up-only arrangement works best when the owner can credibly deliver that service for the whole lease, nights and weekends included.
