Count everything the vacant house costs you each month, then multiply by the months you are willing to wait. That total is the real price of holding out for a sale, and it is usually larger than owners guess.

The monthly list: the mortgage payment, property taxes, insurance, utilities, HOA dues, and landscaping or pool service. Add a repair cushion, and if you rent instead, add vacancy time between tenants and management costs. Then subtract the rent you would collect to find your net monthly gap.

With both numbers in hand you can compare the cost of waiting for a buyer to the cost of renting, and set a deadline in advance: a maximum number of months on the market or a maximum monthly gap, whichever comes first. Deciding the trigger before you list keeps the choice calm instead of rushed.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice; confirm your situation with a qualified professional.
