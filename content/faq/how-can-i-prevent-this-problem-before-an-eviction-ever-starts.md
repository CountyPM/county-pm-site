Build the record at move-in, long before there's any thought of an eviction. Take move-in photos — and photos at every periodic inspection after that — with the house number clearly in frame, dated, and kept in the tenant's file. If a lockout ever does become necessary, you already have exactly the proof a deputy would ask for, created months or years before anyone had a motive to tamper with anything.

This is the same discipline that protects owners in security-deposit disputes and habitability claims: a dated photo taken as routine practice, with nothing riding on it at the time, is far more credible than the same photo produced for the first time on lockout day. Make the house number a standard item on your move-in and inspection checklist and this scenario stops being something you have to think about.
