It can give an eligible owner more room to move rent toward market — but only if the exemption was properly perfected. A single-family home or condominium not owned by a corporation, REIT, or corporate-member LLC can be exempt from the AB 1482 rent cap, which otherwise limits increases to the lower of 5% plus regional CPI or 10% a year.

The catch is the notice. The exemption applies only if the owner served the tenant the specific written exemption language required by Civil Code §1946.2(e)(8)(B). Without that notice, the property defaults to the rent cap no matter who owns it.

Owners who perfected the exemption have more latitude to raise rent to absorb rising insurance premiums, still subject to notice timing and the once-per-12-months rule. Many Ventura County rentals are single-family homes, so this exemption is worth confirming property by property. Verify your exemption language and any increase against the statute or with counsel before you rely on it.
