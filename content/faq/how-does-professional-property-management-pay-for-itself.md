It typically pays for itself through shorter vacancies, stronger tenants, and fewer turnovers. The savings are real but they depend on the property and the manager, so treat this as a way to weigh the fee, not a guarantee.

Tenants who feel well served tend to renew, and every avoided turnover saves cleaning, repainting, marketing, and weeks of lost rent. Good screening reduces the odds of a costly problem tenancy, and repairs done right the first time cost less than repeat visits from the wrong vendor. The honest test is to compare the management fee against your likely vacancy days, turnover costs, and repair costs with and without a manager, using your own numbers.
