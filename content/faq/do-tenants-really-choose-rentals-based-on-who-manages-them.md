Yes, and the strongest applicants are often the ones who ask. Former homeowners and renters coming off a difficult owner-managed lease frequently want to know who will handle repairs and emergencies before they commit to a home.

Some will pass on an otherwise ideal rental if the answer is an owner with no clear process. That is not about the house or the rent; it is about whether problems get fixed promptly and professionally. If you are competing for top applicants, how the property will be managed is part of what you are offering, so be prepared to answer it clearly at the showing.
