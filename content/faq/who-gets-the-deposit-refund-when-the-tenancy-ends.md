In our practice, the deposit refund and the itemized statement go to all of the tenants on the rental agreement together. How that money is then divided among the roommates is a matter for the roommates, not the landlord.

There's a reason for doing it this way. Roommates often contribute to a deposit unequally, or settle shares among themselves when someone moves out mid-tenancy, and the owner rarely knows the details. Paying the tenants named on the agreement, rather than trying to reconstruct who paid what, keeps the owner out of a dispute between roommates.

The timing rules don't change because there are several tenants. Under California Civil Code §1950.5, the landlord has 21 days after the tenants vacate to return the deposit or send an itemized statement of deductions. Tenants who want the refund to arrive smoothly should agree among themselves in advance how to split it and give a forwarding address.
