A roommate can propose a replacement, but the replacement should apply and be screened like any other applicant before moving in. Only after approval should the new person be added to the rental agreement, and the departing roommate removed, in writing and signed by everyone.

The arrangement that causes trouble is the informal swap: one roommate moves out, a friend moves in, and nobody tells the landlord. The owner now has an unscreened occupant who isn't on the agreement, and a former tenant who may believe they're no longer responsible. Neither side is protected.

From the owner's side, there are no exceptions for "a friend of ours." Run the same application, the same income and credit review and the same landlord references you'd require of a stranger, and apply your criteria consistently. From the tenant's side, a properly approved replacement is what actually gets the departing roommate off the agreement.
