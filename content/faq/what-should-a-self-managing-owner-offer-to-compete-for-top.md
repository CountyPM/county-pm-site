At a minimum, offer a roster of licensed, insured vendors, a written emergency procedure with a contact who actually answers, and an online rent payment option. Those three items are what stronger applicants tend to ask about.

The real test is delivering all of it consistently for the entire lease, including nights, weekends, and vacations. A promise you cannot keep when you are traveling is worse than not making it. If you cannot cover that reliably, a lease-up-only arrangement or full management may serve you better than self-managing.
