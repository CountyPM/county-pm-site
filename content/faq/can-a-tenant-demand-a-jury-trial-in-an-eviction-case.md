Yes. A tenant defending an unlawful detainer (eviction) lawsuit can ask for a jury trial, and a tenant who qualifies for a fee waiver can have the jury fee deposit covered. The right comes from the California rule that fact disputes in an action to recover real property are tried by a jury unless the parties waive it.

For an owner, the practical effect is time and cost. A bench trial can be set quickly. After a request to set the case for trial, the court is supposed to hold it within 20 days, but a jury demand adds scheduling, jury selection, and more attorney hours, and crowded court calendars and continuances often push a contested case well past that target.

That is one reason we put so much weight on getting the notice and the paperwork right the first time. A defective three-day notice gives a tenant's attorney something to argue before a jury ever sits. Plan for a contested case taking months, not weeks, and talk to an eviction attorney early if a tenant demands a jury.
