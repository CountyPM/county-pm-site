It is a complimentary analysis for a Ventura County owner deciding whether to sell, rent, or hold a home. It covers what your home would likely rent for today based on current Ventura County rental activity, your monthly cash flow after the mortgage, taxes, insurance, and a realistic reserve, and how much loan principal your tenant pays down each month.

It also compares your holding cost to the price reduction you are being asked to take, shows the date your three-year Section 121 window closes so the plan has an exit from day one, and describes the lease setup that keeps your sale options open. You can bring the result to your tax professional and your lender.

Every home's numbers are different, so the point is a decision based on your rent, loan, tax position, and deadline rather than a general rule.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice; confirm your situation with a qualified professional.
