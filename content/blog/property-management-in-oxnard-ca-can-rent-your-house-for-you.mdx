Before you sell your house, consider renting it for a second income stream. With the right property management in Oxnard, CA, you can receive recurring income by renting your home instead of selling. Granted, there are a number of headaches involved with being a landlord. That is why you want to choose a trusted company with experienced and knowledgeable property managers.

The best property managers will handle the day-to-day operations of managing your rental property, including:

- Inspecting your vacant property and preparing it for rent as quickly as possible.
- Listing and advertising your property for rent.
- Finding and thoroughly screening tenants.
- Responding to tenant questions and requests for maintenance.
- Hiring and scheduling maintenance workers (gardeners, plumbers, etc.).
- Collecting rent and handling evictions when necessary.
- Providing detailed accounting and paying you promptly.
- Additional services, such as paying property taxes or mortgage payments, if requested.

The right property management in Oxnard, CA, can make your life as a landlord much easier. They will do all the work, and you get monthly payments. Your property manager should have reasonable fees for the services they provide and no conflicts of interest. Beware of companies that also own, or have an interest in, the contractors that they hire to work on your property, or that upcharge for these services. When you are ready to start earning an income from your home or condo, call a trusted property manager to discuss property management in Oxnard, CA, for your home.
